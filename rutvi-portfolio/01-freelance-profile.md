## 4. Suggested Rate Strategy

You're new to these platforms, so the goal for the **first 4–6 weeks** is **reviews and ratings**, not maximum pay. Then climb fast.

| Stage | Upwork (hourly) | Per article (500–800 words) | Kolabtree |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Launch** (0 reviews) | $15–20/hr | $25–40 | — (needs portfolio first) |
| **After 3–5 good reviews** | $25–35/hr | $50–80 | apply at $40–50/hr |
| **Established** (10+ reviews) | $40–55/hr | $90–150 | $50–80/hr |

**Tip:** On Upwork, bid on small fixed-price gigs first ($30–50) to bank reviews quickly. On Fiverr, create a "Gig": *"I will write a well-researched medical or health article."* On Kolabtree, wait until you have 2–3 portfolio pieces (you'll have them after this).
